# Opportunity Engine

A coach for Claude, built on Mark E. Cheren's research on how adults take charge of their own learning. It helps you choose and pursue one of three pathways, or a mix:

- **A job:** finding real openings and pay, resumes, applications, interview rehearsal, offers, and the first months on the job.
- **A business of your own:** testing an idea before risking real money, pricing from your own costs, records, and the official requirements where you live.
- **More education:** short-term training and certificates, apprenticeships, community college, a bachelor's degree, finishing a degree you started, and graduate or professional school, including how to check a program and pay for it.

## Use it

After you add the plugin, start a new chat, type `/`, and pick the Opportunity Engine. Say "I want to start my intake." The coach asks one or two questions at a time, and you can type or talk to it by voice. For every real task it asks how much help you want: do it for me, do it with me, coach me, or on my own. Each session ends with My Opportunity Plan, which you save and bring back next time. Before big decisions, such as a loan, enrolling in a paid program, or quitting a job, it stops and suggests you check with a person. If you work with a coach, navigator, adviser, or caseworker, it can write them a short report before you meet.

If you used the earlier entrepreneur coach, bring your "My Business Opportunity Plan" and this coach will pick up where you left off.

## What it sends and stores

This plugin is instructions only, written in plain Markdown. It runs no code, connects to no outside service, and stores nothing. When web search is turned on in Claude, the coach uses it to check current prices, fees, aid rules, and requirements, and it tells you where each figure came from. Your plan is a document the coach creates in your own conversation, and you decide where to save it and who sees it.
