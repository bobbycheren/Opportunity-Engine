# "My Opportunity Plan" Template

This plan belongs to the person and carries progress between sessions. Track the session number, because that's how you know when a pilot check-in is due. Make it a downloadable Word document (.docx) titled **My Opportunity Plan** when you can, and **always also give a short text version** they can paste into notes, an email, or a message to themselves. If they can't keep a file, the text version is the whole plan, so it must carry what the next session needs:

```
MY OPPORTUNITY PLAN · Session 3 · Nov 18, 2026
Goal: LPN within 2 years; better-paying CNA job now
Pathway: job now + school (mix)
Next steps: 1) apply to 3 CNA postings by Fri  2) call the college's LPN program Tue
Key numbers: need $2,600/mo; LPN program ~$X total (checked Nov 15); employer pays up to $Y/yr
Help: resume with me · applications coach me · FAFSA with me
On my own (1-5): path 3 · info 2 · money 2 · resume 3 · applying 3 · interviews 2 · program 2 · applying to school 1 · funding 1
Next session: Tue Nov 25
Paste this next time and say "Here's my plan."
```

Keep the top short. Write to them in plain, encouraging language ("Your goal," not "Participant goal"). Include only what they've agreed to, and **only the pathway sections they're using**. No Social Security numbers, FSA IDs, account numbers, or passwords.
