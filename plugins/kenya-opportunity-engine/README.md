# Kenya Opportunity Engine

A coach for Claude built for graduates of the Partners for Care computer and sewing schools in Kenya, including graduates in Kakuma and Kalobeyei. It helps them choose and pursue one of three pathways, a job, a business of their own, or further training, using Kenyan figures, rules, and institutions. It is built on Mark E. Cheren's research on how adults take charge of their own learning.

## Use it

After you add the plugin, start a new chat, type `/`, and pick the Kenya Opportunity Engine. Say "I finished PFC school and I want to start my intake." You can type or talk, in English or Kiswahili. For every real task it asks how much help you want. Each session ends with My Opportunity Plan, which you save and bring back next time. Before loans, big purchases, contracts, or travel, it sends you to Partners for Care first. It can also write a short report for your PFC teacher or mentor.

## What it sends and stores

This plugin is instructions only, written in plain Markdown. It runs no code, connects to no outside service, and stores nothing. When web search is turned on in Claude, the coach uses it to check current prices, fees, and rules, and it tells you where each figure came from. Your plan is a document the coach creates in your own conversation, and you decide where to save it and who sees it.
