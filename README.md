# Opportunity Engine Coaches

Free AI coaches for Claude, built on Mark E. Cheren's research on how adults take charge of their own learning. Each coach runs a short intake, asks how much help the person wants for each task, builds a plan the person owns, and eases its help off as the person grows.

| Plugin | For |
|---|---|
| `opportunity-engine` | United States: choosing and pursuing a job, a business of your own, or more education (training, apprenticeships, college, finishing a degree, graduate school) |
| `kenya-opportunity-engine` | Partners for Care graduates in Kenya: a job, a business, or further training |

## Add them in Claude

In claude.ai or the Claude desktop app, go to **Customize > Plugins > Add > Add marketplace** and enter this repository's address. Then add the coach you want. It also appears in the Claude phone app on the same account.

Each plugin is instructions only. None runs code, connects to an outside service, or stores anything.
